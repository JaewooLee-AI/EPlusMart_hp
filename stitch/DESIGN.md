## 1. Color Palette & Atmospheric Polarity
- **Primary Brand Canvas (Mart Red)**: `#D70C18` (Deep Crimson Accent: `#B80812`)
- **Editorial Surface (Pure White)**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Subtle Secondary Surface**: `#F7F7F8`
- **Primary Ink (Deep Black)**: `#111111`
- **Muted Metadata Ink**: `#767676`
- **Hairline Structural Divider**: `#E5E5E7` (on white), `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)` (on red)
- **Fresh Accent (Popup / Eco Badge)**: `#0A5C36`

## 2. Typography & Scannable Hierarchy
- **Display Condensed (English Headers & Numbers)**: `'Hanken Grotesk'`, `'Pretendard Variable'`, sans-serif (`font-weight: 800~900`, `letter-spacing: -0.04em`, `line-height: 0.88`)
- **Korean Editorial & Body**: `'Pretendard Variable'`, `'Noto Sans KR'`, sans-serif
- **Hero Giant Wordmark (`EPLUS MART`)**: `clamp(4.25rem, 15.5vw, 14.5rem)`
- **Feature Overlay Display (`ALWAYS FRESH` / `SHOPPING ALL DAY` / `ALWAYS EASY`)**: `clamp(2.5rem, 4.8vw, 4.5rem)`
- **Spec Counter Numerals (`47,878` / `2` / `260`)**: `clamp(3.25rem, 6vw, 5.25rem)` in `#D70C18`

## 3. Structural Grid & Section Rhythm
1. **Top Navigation Bar**: Full-bleed `#D70C18` header with condensed white `EPLUS MART` wordmark, 5 GNB links, `전용 앱 바로가기` white block CTA, and menu drawer button.
2. **Hero Section (`sec-visual`)**: Solid `#D70C18` top canvas, floating top-right promotional layer popup (`오늘 하루동안 보지 않기` + 3-slide switcher), edge-to-edge white `EPLUS MART` display title, Korean slogan, `SCROLL DOWN ↓`, and full-bleed store/freshness visual.
3. **Spec Counters & Asymmetric Intro (`sec-intro`)**: Pure white canvas with 3-column hairline-divided red counters (`47,878 세대`, `2 개점`, `260 만원+`) and asymmetric 12-column corporate vision copy + `VIEW MORE`.
4. **`. FEATURES` (`sec-features`)**: 3 tall editorial photo cards (`ALWAYS FRESH`, `SHOPPING ALL DAY`, `ALWAYS EASY`) with bold white condensed English overlay titles and Korean descriptions below.
5. **`. BRAND` (`sec-brand`)**: Full-bleed `#D70C18` section with 5-cell white-hairline grid (`01 EPLUS FRESH` ~ `05 B2B WHOLESALE`), wide panoramic farm harvest banner, and giant outlined `BEST SHOPPING EXPERIENCE` display text.
6. **`. STORE INFORMATION` (`sec-store`)**: Interactive split layout — left branch selector (`평내 본점` / `퇴계원점`) & specification table; right interactive map canvas with custom red marker pin, top-right branch dropdown, and Naver/Kakao map links.
7. **`. EVENT` (`sec-event`)**: 2-column horizontal poster cards with `[지점명] 제목`, date range, summary, and click-to-open Digital Flyer Modal.
8. **`. RECRUITMENT & B2B` (`sec-recruit-b2b`)**: Recruitment board + 30-second non-login application form (2026 minimum wage +20.5% comparison, 5-day destruction & talent pool checkboxes, Hiring Procedure Act Article 11 notice) + B2B wholesale supply form.
9. **Footer**: Full-bleed `#D70C18` footer with giant `EPLUS MART` wordmark, pill CTA buttons, and (주)피케이유통 corporate metadata.
